PRIVACY POLICY

How this public website handles information.

This policy describes the public Annapurna Agentic Solutions website, including the Pramana information pages. It does not describe any separately operated customer product or service.

Information you choose to send

You can use the Pramana DPDP discussion check and print your notes without giving us your name or email. If you request an email copy, we receive the name, email address, profession or role, optional business name and size, business context, and your 14 selected answers. Please do not enter personal details about other people in these fields.

We use your email address to send a six-digit verification code. Your answers and contact details are kept briefly in the verification service while you enter the code. The temporary verification record expires after 10 minutes if you do not finish. After successful verification and email delivery, we remove it. We record your name, email address and request time separately to deliver the requested notes and limit repeat emails. If you separately choose “contact me about Pramana”, we keep the business details you supplied and your contact choice in a separate contact list. The current check does not write your question answers, a score or a legal conclusion to either list. Records from an earlier version of the check may contain earlier report fields.

An address that received notes in the previous 24 hours cannot receive another set during that period. The email service and website host process data to deliver this feature. The separate “contact me about Pramana” choice is optional and starts unchecked. If you select it, we may contact you about Pramana; otherwise we use your details only to provide and manage the email you requested. You can request access, correction or deletion of the contact record using the address below.

If you email us directly, your message and contact details are processed to receive and respond to that enquiry. Your mail provider may also process it. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information in an initial product enquiry. The check does not make a legal or product-eligibility decision.

Browser storage and website operation

The wider Annapurna website may store navigation preferences in local storage on your device to support page navigation. Those preferences are held in your browser and are not sent by the site’s navigation code to a Pramana assessment service. You can clear local storage through your browser settings.

The website is hosted using a third-party hosting service. Technical request data may be processed to deliver, maintain and protect the site. The hosting provider’s terms and privacy information apply to its processing.

Requests and contact

For questions about this policy or a message you sent us, contact contact@annapurnaagenticsolutions.com. We will review the request and respond through an appropriate channel. This contact address is not presented as a statutory grievance-resolution service or as a promise of a DPDP statutory deadline.

Scope and updates

This policy was reviewed on 24 September 2026. It covers the public website only. Product deployments or services, if separately provided, require their own applicable terms and privacy information.

DPDP commencement is phased. The rights and obligations relevant to a particular request depend on the provision’s effective status and the facts.

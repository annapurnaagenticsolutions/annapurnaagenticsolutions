PRIVACY POLICY

How we handle your personal data.

This policy explains what personal data Annapurna Agentic Solutions collects on this website, why we collect it, who processes it, how long we keep it, and the rights you have under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Last updated: September 2026.

SECTION 1

Who we are

Annapurna Agentic Solutions is the Data Fiduciary responsible for the personal data collected on this website. We are based in Bengaluru, India.

Contact for privacy matters: annapurnaagenticsolutions@zohomail.in

SECTION 2

What personal data we collect

We collect personal data only when you choose to use the DPDP compliance check or contact us. We do not collect data from passive browsing.

DPDP compliance check (lead gate)

When you submit the compliance check form, we collect:

  • Name — so we can address you in the report email
  • Work email address — used to send your verification code and report. We do not accept personal email domains (Gmail, Yahoo, etc.) — this is a B2B tool.
  • Organisation, role, company size, sector, budget range — for lead qualification so we can tailor follow-up
  • Compliance score, grade, and section results — the summary of your assessment, so we can reference it in conversation
  • Source page URL and UTM parameters (if present) — to understand how you found us

What stays in your browser

Your questionnaire answers are processed entirely in your browser. They are not sent to our servers. Only the summary fields listed above (score, grade, section results) and the form fields you explicitly fill in are transmitted.

Navigation memory (local storage)

Our site stores a short navigation trail in your browser’s local storage to remember which product pages you’ve visited. This data never leaves your device. You can clear it at any time using the “Reset local memory” button in the site footer.

Contact form

If you use the contact form, we collect the name, email, and message you provide.

SECTION 3

Why we collect it and the legal basis

We process your personal data under the following purposes, each based on your consent (DPDP Act, Section 6):

  • Sending your compliance report — you requested it, and we need your email to deliver it.
  • Lead qualification and follow-up — to understand whether Pramana is a fit for your organisation and to contact you about it. You consented to this in the form.
  • Service improvement — aggregated, de-identified assessment scores help us understand which compliance gaps are common. No individual is identifiable in this aggregation.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by emailing annapurnaagenticsolutions@zohomail.in. Withdrawal does not affect processing that was already completed before you withdrew.

SECTION 4

Who processes your data

We do not sell or share your personal data with any third party for their own purposes. We use the following processors, each bound by contract to process data only on our instructions:

ProcessorWhat they doWhereData they see
Cloudflare Web hosting, D1 database, KV storage Global edge network (data stored in Cloudflare’s infrastructure) All submitted form data (stored in D1 after verification)
Resend Transactional email delivery (verification code) United States Your name and email address (for the OTP email only)

OTP data is short-lived. Your one-time verification code is stored in Cloudflare KV with a 10-minute expiry. It is automatically deleted after that window or once used — whichever comes first.

Resend is a US-based processor. By submitting the compliance check form, you acknowledge that your name and email address will be transmitted to Resend’s servers in the United States for the sole purpose of sending the verification email. No other personal data is shared with Resend.

SECTION 5

How long we keep your data

  • OTP in KV: 10 minutes, then automatically deleted.
  • Verified lead data in D1: Retained for up to 24 months from your last interaction with us. If you request a re-send within that period, the clock resets.
  • Contact form submissions: Retained for up to 12 months.
  • Navigation memory (local storage): Persists on your device until you clear it or use the “Reset local memory” button.

We do not retain your questionnaire answers — those never leave your browser.

SECTION 6

Your rights under the DPDP Act, 2023

As a Data Principal, you have the following rights regarding the personal data we hold about you:

  • Right to Access (Section 11): You can ask for a summary of what personal data we hold about you, who we’ve shared it with, and why.
  • Right to Correction and Erasure (Section 12): You can ask us to correct inaccurate data or delete your data once it is no longer needed for the purpose it was collected.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal (Section 13): You can file a complaint if you believe we have mishandled your data.
  • Right to Nominate (Section 14): You can nominate another individual to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent (Section 6): You can withdraw your consent at any time. Processing that was already completed before withdrawal is not affected.

To exercise any of these rights, email annapurnaagenticsolutions@zohomail.in. We will respond within 30 days.

SECTION 7

How we protect your data

Your data is transmitted over HTTPS. The D1 database and KV namespace are access-controlled via Cloudflare’s platform, with secrets stored as encrypted environment variables. No plaintext secrets are committed to our repositories.

No security measure is perfect. If a data breach occurs that is likely to cause you harm, we will notify you and the Data Protection Board of India within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, as required by DPDP Section 8(6) and Rule 7.

SECTION 8

Children’s data

This website is a B2B product. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 18. The lead gate rejects personal email domains and requires a work email, which is inconsistent with a child user. If you believe a child’s data has been collected, contact us immediately and we will delete it.

SECTION 9

Grievance officer

Under DPDP Section 10(3), we have designated a grievance officer to handle data protection complaints. For all privacy grievances:

Email: annapurnaagenticsolutions@zohomail.in
Response time: Within 30 days of receiving your complaint.

SECTION 10

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our practices evolve or as the DPDP Rules are finalised. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the website after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

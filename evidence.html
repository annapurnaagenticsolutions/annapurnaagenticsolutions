Why we publish this

Most vendors ask you to trust their claims. We'd rather show you the path to each one.

What's verified, what's source-local, and where the evidence runs out. Conflicts between sources are surfaced rather than silently resolved toward the more promotional reading.

If something here looks wrong, incomplete, or missing a source — tell us → and we'll correct it publicly. That's the point of keeping this separate from the marketing pages.

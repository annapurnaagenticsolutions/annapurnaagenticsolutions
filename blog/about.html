About

The agentic AI space is full of demos and short on engineering. This blog is about the gaps — governance, RAG limits, why SLMs matter, failure economics, and the unglamorous work that makes agents deployable.

We're a small team in India building open-source tools — AXON (a typed DSL for AI agents) and AgentOps Mesh (a governance control plane). But this blog isn't about those tools. It's about the problems they solve, the patterns we see across the industry, and the questions that don't have easy answers yet.

What we cover:

  • Ideas & Views — agent design, governance gaps, failure economics, RAG limitations, SLMs vs LLMs, and where agentic AI is actually going
  • Open Source & Building — the engineering behind agentic solutions, what we built, what broke, and what we learned
  • Education & STEM — AI literacy, science education, and building tools that work for the next generation

Our voice: Opinionated but not dogmatic. Concrete examples, not abstract theory. Ideas first, products second. We write about what the hype won't tell you — because that's where the real work is.

SECURITY

Vulnerability Disclosure Policy

We take security seriously. If you have found a potential vulnerability in Annapurna Agentic Solutions’ website, systems, or the Pramana governance engine, please tell us responsibly. We will acknowledge, investigate, and respond.

How to report

Send a report to security@annapurnaagenticsolutions.com. Please include:

  • A description of the issue and where you found it
  • Steps to reproduce it reliably
  • The potential impact as you understand it
  • Any supporting screenshots, logs, or proof-of-concept (no need to exploit — the report is enough)

We do not currently have a bug bounty programme, but we will acknowledge every good-faith report and keep you updated on its resolution.

What to expect

  • Acknowledgement within 5 business days of your report
  • Initial triage and severity assessment within 10 business days
  • Resolution or interim mitigation communicated to you once applied
  • We will credit you publicly (with your consent) when the issue is resolved

Scope

In scope:

  • This website and its subpages
  • Pramana’s API surface when deployed by Annapurna Agentic Solutions for demonstration
  • Any exposure of personal data, credentials, or internal configuration

Out of scope:

  • Customer-hosted Pramana deployments (those are operated by the customer; they are responsible for their own infrastructure)
  • Automated scanning results without a proof-of-concept
  • Issues requiring physical access to hardware we do not operate

Good faith

We ask that you:

  • Give us reasonable time to respond before public disclosure
  • Do not access or modify data belonging to other users
  • Do not disrupt the availability of the site for others

In return, we commit to:

  • Treat your report in good faith
  • Not pursue legal action against researchers acting within this policy
  • Correct valid findings promptly

Our own incident response

Annapurna Agentic Solutions maintains a written incident response plan. In the event of a security incident affecting personal data we hold (such as website lead data), we commit to notifying affected individuals and the Data Protection Board of India within the timeframes required by the DPDP Act 2023.

The Pramana system is designed so that customer-hosted deployments are fully self-contained — your governance evidence records and personal data never leave your infrastructure. Annapurna does not hold your operational data.

## Art Direction

The UI should use the hero as a full-bleed atmospheric layer with a dark gradient overlay so narrative text remains highly legible. Portraits are cropped into small, rounded story cards. Slime art is presented as luminous collection tokens with locked silhouettes for creatures not yet discovered.

# Vidya Yantra — Season II: The Open Almanac

## Season premise

After the Living Survey publishes a variance beside an earlier route, the campaign turns outward. **The Open Almanac** follows Ila and Tara through communities that must use seasonal records without mistaking them for permanent authority. Each chapter asks how observation, repair, story, terrain, and public responsibility can remain available to the next reader.

This is an original fictional season shaped by the project’s plural knowledge-traditions approach. It does not adapt a sacred narrative, simulate ritual, or treat one historical period as universal.

## Reusable chapter framework

Every season chapter uses the existing Rasa Engine campaign contract: a distinct Babylon.js biome, three inspectable field stations, a central synthesis or comparison instrument, a guide or community interlocutor, one public-care choice, a carried technique or material, a six-season replay variant, Chronicle/map/codex records, camera and ambient support, and reduced-motion/high-contrast/large-text affordances. The player’s prior lineage record remains readable but never dictates the new chapter’s outcome.

## Launch slice and verification

The first launch slice comprises the dedicated Chapter XI plate, the playable **Lineage Chamber**, enhanced New Journey+ seasonal dialogue and challenge ladder, then Chapter XIII’s horizon and scaffold. The lineage chamber must visibly show four separately inspectable layers in world space, route threads that do not merge until the player elects a public notation, and a return path to the New Journey+ challenge ladder. The visual QA target is the registered Living Survey plate in `ASSETS.md`.

## Visual target

The material language remains deep indigo, parchment, copper, jade, Yantra Saffron, and star blue. The Lineage Chamber is a structured survey basin rather than a temple or a throne room: four physical line layers, three field stations, a modest Surveyor of Returns, and a practical HUD. The season gradually moves from contained basin to wind terrace, workshop, grassland, forest floor, caravan route, river assembly, and shared observatory.

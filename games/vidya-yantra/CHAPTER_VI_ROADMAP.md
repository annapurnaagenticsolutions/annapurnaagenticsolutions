# Chapter VI — The Listening Estuary

The Archive Shard reaches the coast, where a tidal observatory must translate salinity, current, and habitat evidence into a warning the estuary communities can use. The chapter converts the prior public-warning record into a stewardship choice rather than treating the coast as a simple destination.

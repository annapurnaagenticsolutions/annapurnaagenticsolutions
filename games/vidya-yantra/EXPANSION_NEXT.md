# Vidya Yantra — Next Integrated Feature Pass

## Feature Dependency Map

The new systems are designed as one adventure loop rather than isolated additions. Camera behavior expresses confidence and danger, the companion makes the river road feel social, seasons and time turn the same paths into different readings, and the Rasa encounter converts accumulated practices into a visible test.

|---|---|---|---|
| Camera language | Orbit controls, close focus, route viewpoints, pulse emphasis | Preferred camera mode | Focus framing on alignment stages |

## Chapter III Encounter Logic

The Engine asks the traveller to align three rings in order: **observe**, **hold**, and **share**. The first stage uses Star-thread Sight to find the route; the second uses measured movement and focus timing while pressure rises; the third asks the traveller to stand within a shelter anchor before making the final choice.

The stability meter drops only during the pressure stage. A well-timed focus pulse restores stability, while reaching a shelter anchor recovers it more reliably. The encounter remains nonviolent: it evaluates practice, movement, and responsibility rather than defeating an enemy.

## New Persistent Technique

Completing the Engine encounter grants **Resonance Shelter**, an expanded form of Shelter Weave. It marks safe circular spaces, stabilizes environmental mechanisms, and creates an option in later companion dialogue. The technique record includes the carried stance and the current Tara bond, allowing the journal to describe *why* the Engine recognized the player.

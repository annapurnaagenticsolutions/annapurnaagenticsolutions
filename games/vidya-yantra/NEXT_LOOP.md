# Vidya Yantra — Road Completion and Rasa Engine Loop

## Chapter II Resolution

Nadi Corridor resolves when Ila charts all three public routes, speaks with Tara Vaidyi, and makes a stance-led decision at the Open Crossing. The reward is not a trophy. The player receives a **Route Seal**, a new **Rasa Engine destination**, and a public consequence recorded in the Traveller’s Chronicle.

| Carried stance | Route consequence | Closing note |
|---|---|---|
| Viveka | The flood-line is marked carefully, making the crossing safer to read. | “A correct line can still leave someone out; keep looking.” |
| Sahas | A risky temporary ferry opens for the isolated floodplain. | “Courage becomes useful when it returns for the next traveler.” |
| Karuna | Shared water access is protected before trade traffic resumes. | “Care makes a route longer only when we ignore who was carrying the cost.” |

The first three route markers provide the material loop in miniature. Each pulse pickup grants one map fragment, while the route choice awards the remaining practical materials. The player combines all four in the field journal’s **Compass Repair** action.

## Chapter III Readiness Gate

The Rasa Engine opens only when the traveller has a repaired compass, at least one guide practice, and a Route Seal. This is a preparation gate, not a grind gate: the player sees precisely what is missing in the journal.

Chapter III begins at the **Silent Courtyard**, a collapsed observatory where the repaired compass makes the star-thread visible. The first alignment challenge uses three inputs earned from prior learning: a map fragment identifies the correct ring, Rainward Pulse awakens it, and a carried moral stance determines the Engine’s first answer.

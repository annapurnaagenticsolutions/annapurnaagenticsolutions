# Chapter V — The High-Ridge Warning Archive

## Purpose

The High-Ridge Warning Archive turns the previous chapters’ question into a playable test: **can evidence, calm movement, and shelter become a warning that reaches people before danger does?** The Archive is not a combat arena. It is a mountain observatory where the player compares signals, steers wind-rain traces, and carries a public decision into a new landscape.

## Experience Rules

The player’s path runs diagonally from a lower-left refuge terrace toward an upper-right archive lens. Copper marks active evidence axes; jade marks usable shelter; parchment represents records that can be revised; deep indigo is only the sky behind these mineral materials. Every progress step updates the objective, a world object, and the Chronicle.

## Verification

Chapter V must be reachable through `#archive` and the field map after the Monsoon public decision. Each lens must respond to a focus pulse, and resolving the Ledger must visibly award the Archive Shard, update the timeline, unlock the mastery threshold, and expose the Chapter VI horizon card.

# Vidya Yantra — Post-Rasa Journey Roadmap

## Fifteen-Feature Progression Logic

The post-Rasa loop turns the newly earned **Resonance Shelter** technique into social and environmental usefulness. Companion quests create emotional stakes, the field map creates navigational clarity, and the Monsoon Observatory becomes the next destination because it needs the exact kind of responsible practice that the Engine recognized.

| Layer | Features | Dependency | Reward |
|---|---|---|---|
| Companion | Tara, Aruna, and Laya field quests; milestone conversations; bond records | Rasa Engine completed | Bond rewards, new field notes, companion calls |
| Navigation | Field map, fast travel, destination cards, reactive route conditions | At least one route destination unlocked | Clear next activity and place-based state |
| Chapter IV | Briefing, advanced Rainward Pulse, stormfront traversal, forecast advantage | Resonance Shelter and Monsoon route | New Prana technique and access to later observatory challenge |
| Long-term growth | Discipline mastery and end-session Chronicle summary | Completed quests and encounters | Visible direction for the next play session |

## Companion Quest Order

Tara’s **Missing Sounding** opens first because it turns a relationship into a practical river task. Aruna’s evidence quest follows after the player marks a conflicting route line. Laya’s stability practice appears at the edge of the stormfront. Each quest progresses one branch while showing that Shastra, Prana, and Seva are useful beyond a training yard.

## Chapter IV Preparation

The Monsoon Observatory asks why a moving current cannot be solved with a static chart. The forecast board previews the condition, but the player must use Rainward Pulse to redirect a current, then use Resonance Shelter to keep the resulting route safe. This makes the new Chapter IV objective a continuation of learning rather than a new unrelated minigame.

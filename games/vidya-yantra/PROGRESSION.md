# Vidya Yantra — Character, Skills, and Journey Progression

## The Apprentice and the World

The player character is **Ila**, a young map-copyist from a river settlement whose family once maintained practical flood charts for nearby villages. After a sudden pattern of starfall disrupts the river routes, Ila arrives at Ashraya Vana carrying a damaged copper field compass and an unfinished question: why do the night charts and the river behave as though they are answering one another?

Ila begins as observant rather than exceptional. Their strength is that they can hold several perspectives at once: a scholar’s pattern, a craftsperson’s constraint, and a traveler’s risk. Rishi Aruna recognizes this attention; Muni Laya tests whether it survives practice; Raja Somavrat asks whom it serves.

## Skill Constellation

| Discipline | Learning fantasy | Skill 1 | Skill 2 | Skill 3 | Practical effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Shastra | Read patterns without mistaking them for certainty | **Star-thread Sight** | Route Inference | Yantra Reading | Reveals links between clues, village needs, and puzzle anchors. |
| Astra | Practice deliberate action and safe form | **Measured Step** | Staff Guard | Resonant Strike | Improves traversal timing, training encounters, and pulse control. |
| Prana | Balance breath, motion, and changing conditions | **Rainward Pulse** | Wind Balance | Stillwater Focus | Alters environmental nodes, carries through rain, and steadies active instruments. |
| Seva | Treat repair and care as real forms of power | **Shelter Weave** | Field Repair | Shared Provision | Opens community solutions, restores damaged paths, and protects companions. |

The first skill in each branch is a visible technique. Later skills extend it rather than replacing it. The player’s **Viveka**, **Sahas**, and **Karuna** values remain an ethical lens: they determine which response, solution, or benefit becomes most natural, not a simple right-or-wrong meter.

## Original Magic Principles

The game’s magic is an original system called **Vidya Resonance**. It is not a list of spells. A technique works only when the player understands its material condition and ethical use.

> **Star-thread Sight** connects observed relationships as fine lines of light. **Rainward Pulse** tunes unstable water, wind, and yantra nodes through measured rhythm. **Shelter Weave** creates temporary zones of repair and safety through care directed at a real need.

## Chapter II: The Road of Many Arts

The Ashram opens onto the **Nadi Corridor**, a river travel map rather than an immediate open world. Ila selects destinations along an illustrated route: Ashraya Vana, **Nadi Bazaar**, the Reedbank Ferry, the Floodplain Orchard, and the silent courtyard that later points toward the Rasa Engine.

Nadi Bazaar is the first playable Chapter II space. Its immediate question is practical: a shifting riverbank has damaged a water measure and made the market’s eastern path unsafe. The player speaks with the river guide **Tara Vaidhi**, chart-maker **Mitra**, and basket maker **Asha**. Each asks for a different kind of attention, and the player must use the skills learned at the Ashram to choose an approach.

## Persistence Contract

The game saves an original **Traveller’s Chronicle** in browser storage. It records unlocked skills, skill ranks, trait values, guide quest stages, current chapter, chosen travel location, and important dialogue stances. The UI should make this state visible as a small field journal rather than a spreadsheet.

# Assets

**Art direction:** An original mythic-science adventure in a sun-baked astronomical courtyard at indigo dusk. Treat copper instrumentation, parchment sandstone, restrained jade, and Yantra Saffron (`#F26B38`) as intentional material signals. The art is a clean, sharp, hand-painted 3D game language with legible silhouettes and restrained effects; it is not a depiction of religious stories or sacred figures.

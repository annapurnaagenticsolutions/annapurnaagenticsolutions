# Vidya Yantra — Cinematic Movement and World-Life Pass

## Camera Language

The camera should feel like an attentive companion rather than a fixed spectator. A **travelling frame** sits slightly behind and above Ila, leads their direction of motion by a small distance, and eases back when an objective or guide is nearby. A **focus frame** briefly tightens during a pulse, dialogue threshold, or objective completion, then returns to a wider travelling angle. Every chapter receives an arrival sweep that glides from a signature landmark toward Ila’s starting position during the first seconds of play.

| World | Arrival emphasis | Movement quality | Focus anchor |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ashraya Vana | Pavilion, practice rings, forest canopy | Calm, low arc, warm dawn | Rishi Aruna and the four study circles |
| Nadi Corridor | River bend, ferry sail, bazaar awnings | Lateral river tracking, buoyant motion | Tara, route markers, and the Open Crossing |
| Silent Courtyard | Tilted pillars, copper rings, star-thread horizon | Slow orbital drift, restrained tension | The central Rasa Engine and recovered measures |

## Character and Environment Priorities

Ila’s game silhouette gains a layered indigo over-tunic, a pale parchment sash, jade belt knot, staff tip, compass glow, and a subtle walking sway. Guide figures gain a readable prop and pose: Aruna’s chart arm, Laya’s measured breath gesture, Somavrat’s civic scroll, and Tara’s rope coil and river stance.

Environmental life remains useful to the player. Leaves move toward hidden wind paths, river ribbons show direction, banners indicate occupied spaces, fireflies and star motes indicate safe or meaningful points, and small story objects reward inspection with concise world notes.

Release contract

What must be true before a download goes public.

Artifact

Release-signed APK or Play listing, reproducible version metadata and a tested install path.

Trust

Privacy policy, Data Safety answers, permissions, support contact and age/category positioning reviewed together.

Evidence

Physical-device QA, crash monitoring and a clear rollback owner. Build success alone does not open a public download.

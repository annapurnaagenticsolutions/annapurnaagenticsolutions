What is CareLink?

CareLink is a voice-first family reminder and learning app designed specifically for Indian households. Set medication reminders for elderly parents with Hindi voice announcements, help kids learn with 52+ educational activities, and keep your family organized—all without sending data to the cloud.

Built for: Adult children caring for elderly parents, parents teaching young kids at home, joint families staying coordinated, budget smartphone users, and Hindi-first users.

Core Features

🏥

Medication Reminders

Loud alarm that bypasses lock screen. Hindi voice: "खाने का समय हो गया है". Night mode (1-4 AM). Voice input: "Add Dad's medicine at 8 AM".

📚

TouchLearn

52 learning activities: colors, shapes, animals, bird math, memory games. Hindi/English bilingual. Streak rewards. Perfect for ages 3-8.

🗣️

Hindi Voice Support

Full Text-to-Speech in Hindi. Voice commands throughout. Designed for users with low digital literacy. Large buttons, simple UI.

🔐

Privacy-First

Zero cloud storage. All data encrypted locally (AES-256). No ads, no tracking. Optional local Wi-Fi sync only.

♿

Accessibility

High contrast mode. Kids XL mode (larger touch targets). Audio-only guidance. Reduced motion option. Elderly-friendly design.

📴

Offline-First

Works without internet. No cloud dependency. Reminders trigger even in airplane mode. Perfect for areas with poor connectivity.

🎯 Main Use Case: Elderly Care

CareLink's medication reminder feature uses production-grade alarm implementation: bypasses lock screen, maximizes volume, loops voice announcement every 5 seconds until dismissed, and disables back button to prevent accidental dismissal. Designed for elderly users who may not hear quiet notifications.

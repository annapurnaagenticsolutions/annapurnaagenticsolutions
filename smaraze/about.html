About Skill X

Empowering the next generation
of STEM pioneers

Skill X is the interactive learning platform presented by Avyan Info Solution for Classes 1–10. It gives students, schools, and tuition centres one class-first home for conceptual understanding, visual tools, step-by-step breakdowns, and diagnostic misconception coaching.

The Authentic 5-Step Learning Ladder

An evidence-informed learning cycle designed to help children explain ideas, not only memorize them.

1

See It

Interactive cause-and-effect visual models — subtraction take-aways, Ohm's law electron drift, force vector arrows.

2

Break It Down

KaTeX equations, color-coded variable cards, formula magic triangles, and live substitution calculators.

3

Why It Works

Underlying physical conservation principles, real-world analogies, and the scientific "why" behind every concept.

4

Solve

Worked examples with progressive step-by-step disclosure to prevent cognitive overload.

5

Practice

Adaptive questions with instant misconception diagnosis explaining why a mistake occurred, with 1-click remediation.

For educators

Designed for classrooms and tuition batches

Use Skill X as a structured practice layer alongside your school or centre's official teaching plan.

🏫

Schools

Teachers can use class-wise paths, visual demonstrations, and shareable lesson assignments to support revision and guided practice.

👩‍🏫

Tuition centres

Tutors can begin with a concept, let learners practise at their own pace, and use review sessions to identify topics needing another explanation.

👪

Families

Parents can choose the child's class, explore lessons together, and keep learning time focused without replacing the official syllabus.

Skill X is an independent study aid. Schools and tuition centres should continue to follow their official syllabus, safeguarding policy, and teacher-led assessment process.

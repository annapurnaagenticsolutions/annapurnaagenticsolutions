Child Safety Charter

Our commitment to creating a distraction-free, ad-free, and developmentally safe learning environment.

🌱 1. A Sanctuary for Young Minds

The internet can be noisy, distracting, and unsafe for young students. Skill X was founded on the conviction that a learning platform must be a quiet, dignified sanctuary where children can focus, experiment, and think deeply without popups, dark patterns, or behavioral manipulation.

🚫 2. Zero Ads, Zero Sponsored Content

We believe advertising has no place in a child's education. Skill X will NEVER:

  • Serve commercial advertisements, banners, or video sponsors.
  • Accept paid product placements or branded content within STEM lessons.
  • Monetize user attention, screen time, or engagement loops.

🧠 3. Age-Appropriate & Developmentally Vetted Content

Every lesson, diagram, and quiz is carefully audited for developmental appropriateness across our three educational tiers:

  • Foundational (Classes 1–4): Concrete, visual, playful learning focusing on everyday wonders, physical counting, and nature observation. Highly abstract or hazard-prone topics are strictly excluded.
  • Preparatory (Classes 5–7): Transitional thinking bridging hands-on experiments with scientific models and basic formulas.
  • Secondary (Classes 8–10): Rigorous scientific inquiry, quantitative equations, experimental proofs, and board exam preparation.

⚡ 4. Digital Well-Being & Safe Experiments

All hands-on activities are non-hazardous, using common kitchen and household objects (paper, spoons, water). Activities involving tools or heat explicitly require adult supervision and include safety warnings.

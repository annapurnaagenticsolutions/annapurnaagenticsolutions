Child Safety & Privacy

Parental Consent & Family Oversight

Understanding how parents authorize, monitor, and guide their child’s digital STEM learning journey.

👨‍👩‍👧 1. Why Parental Consent Matters

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), digital platforms serving children under 18 require a lawful consent basis and appropriate guardian controls. The current build records an authenticated parent-account link; it does not yet provide independent identity, email, or OTP verification, so production paid launch remains gated on that control.

Children are curious, energetic learners. Our intended consent framework gives parents visibility into their child's educational progress while avoiding commercial tracking; the controls remain subject to production verification.

🔗 2. The Multi-Layer Consent Architecture

Skill X provides a granular two-tier consent structure:

  • Core Learning Layer (Essential): Enables account creation, grade-band lesson browsing, step-by-step visualizations, and diagnostic quiz scoring.
  • Communication & Reporting Layer (Optional): Authorizes weekly email summary reports sent to parents and smart spaced-repetition notifications.

Optional layers can be toggled on or off at any moment without affecting access to core lessons.

📱 3. Account Linking & Verification

Parents can connect to their child's profile via a 6-character secure linking code generated in the child's session or by registering directly through the parent enrollment flow. Once linked, parents can:

  • View weekly study time, concepts mastered, and SmartScore trends.
  • Identify areas of misconception highlighted during diagnostic quizzes.
  • Receive weekly progress snapshots via email or exportable reports.

🛑 4. Right to Withdraw & Immediate Data Deletion

Parents can withdraw optional consent and request deletion. The current deletion job removes learning, report, consent, session, and relationship records and writes a durable receipt; legally required minimal financial records may be retained and independent legal review is still required.

DPDP Act 2023 Readiness Notice

Privacy Policy

How Skill X protects learner data, respects parental rights, and upholds statutory child privacy standards.

🛡️ 1. Data Fiduciary Identity & Scope

Skill X is an interactive STEM learning service presented by Avyan Info Solution with operational support from Annapurna Agentic Solutions ("Data Fiduciary", "we", "us", "our").

This policy describes Skill X's intended data practices with reference to the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act, India), the Information Technology Act, 2000, and applicable child-safety guidance. It remains subject to final legal review and operational verification before paid launch.

👶 2. Special Protections for Children (Section 9, DPDP Act)

Current product position: Skill X is designed to process learner data only for STEM learning and account operations. It does not include cross-site tracking, behavioral advertising, or commercial child profiling; final legal and operational review remains required.

Verifiable Parental Consent

The current product records a parent-account linking relationship and keeps optional analytics and voice layers disabled for self-declared under-18 accounts until an independent guardian-verification method is deployed. Legal approval and production verification are required before paid launch.

Zero Targeted Advertising & No Tracking

The current Skill X web build contains no advertising placements or behavioral tracking pixels. No advertising tracking pixels, third-party behavioral analytics, or commercial user fingerprinting scripts are loaded by this build.

📊 3. Categories of Data We Process

  • Account Information: Learner display name, parent or student email address, hashed password, role (Student, Parent, Teacher), and enrolled class band (Classes 1–10).
  • Learning Progress & Mastery: Topics completed, diagnostic quiz responses, spaced review schedules, SmartScore calculations, streak counts, and earned badges.
  • Parent Contact: Parent-account email address used for relationship linking, progress digests, and subscription billing receipts. Independent guardian verification is not yet available.
  • Payment Metadata: Transaction identifiers, order IDs, and GST compliance details managed through Razorpay. Skill X NEVER stores debit/credit card numbers or UPI PINs.

⚖️ 4. Rights of Data Principals (Learners & Parents)

Learners and their parents or guardians may exercise applicable data rights under the DPDP Act 2023; the process and legal basis remain subject to final legal review:

  • Right to Access: View all recorded progress, quiz attempts, and profile information at any time via the Parent Dashboard.
  • Right to Correction & Completion: Update name, enrolled class level, or contact details directly in account settings.
  • Right to Erasure (Withdrawal of Consent): Parents may withdraw consent at any time. When full withdrawal is requested, the deletion job durably removes learning, report, consent, session, and relationship records and records a completion receipt; minimal financial records may be retained where legally required.
  • Right of Grievance Redressal: Contact our designated Grievance Officer. The published response target is subject to staffing and final legal approval.

A gentle feedback loop

Four signals of meaningful progress

Each signal is designed to guide the next helpful step, never to rank children.

🔎01

Explore

Meet a concept with a visual explanation, example or experiment.

🧩02

Practise

Use low-pressure questions to connect the idea to a new situation.

🪴03

Reflect

Notice what feels clear, what needs another try and what question comes next.

🎯04

Grow

Return to the right lesson at the right time and celebrate steady effort.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Clear, transparent policy regarding subscription cancellations, money-back guarantee, and refund processing.

✨ 1. 7-Day Money-Back Guarantee

A 7-calendar-day refund window is offered for subscription purchases, subject to payment verification, the final approved terms, and the support process described below. Refunds are returned to the original payment source; this policy does not make a claim about learning outcomes.

📝 2. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund within the 7-day guarantee window:

  1. Send an email from your registered parent account to contact@smaraze.com.
  2. Include your Order ID (from your invoice receipt) or the registered email address.
  3. Our support team will verify the purchase and initiate the refund within 24 to 48 business hours.

🏦 3. Refund Timelines & Payment Method

Refunds are credited directly back to the original payment source (UPI account, Net Banking, Credit/Debit Card) via our authorized gateway Razorpay.

  • UPI & Net Banking: Typically credited within 2 to 4 business days.
  • Credit & Debit Cards: Typically credited within 5 to 7 business days depending on your issuing bank.

📅 4. Cancellations Beyond 7 Days

Subscriptions cancelled after the initial 7-day period remain active until the end of the paid term (6 months or 1 year) and will not automatically renew. Exceptional circumstances (such as accidental double-payment) will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis by our billing desk.

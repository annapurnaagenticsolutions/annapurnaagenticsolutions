WHAT PRAMANA IS NOT

Four things we say clearly, before you buy.

📜

Not a compliance certificate.

Pramana produces governance evidence — sealed, timestamped records of the decisions your AI systems made and the rules applied at the moment they were made. It does not issue a certificate, an attestation, or a determination that your organisation is DPDP-compliant.

Your compliance assessment score is a self-assessment of readiness based on the information you provide. It is not an independent audit finding and should not be described as one.

If a regulator or buyer asks whether you are “DPDP-certified,” the honest answer is that no such certification currently exists in India — and Pramana does not create one.

⚖️

Not legal advice.

Pramana encodes rules derived from the DPDP Act 2023, the DPDP Rules 2025, and related regulatory sources. It checks AI actions against those rules. It does not advise you on how those rules apply to your specific facts, your contracts, or your disputes.

If a governance decision produces a result you disagree with, the right response is to consult a qualified advocate — not to reconfigure the tool until it produces the answer you prefer.

Pramana's output is evidence of governance decisions, not a legal opinion. Your advocate should review the evidence; they should not rely on the tool as a substitute for their own analysis.

🔍

Not a professional compliance audit.

A professional data protection audit involves an independent qualified practitioner examining your people, processes, technology, and contracts against applicable law. Pramana governs AI decision-making at runtime. These are different things, and neither replaces the other.

Many organisations that use Pramana will still need periodic professional audits — especially those approaching Significant Data Fiduciary designation, which mandates independent data audits.

🚫

Not a substitute for an accountable human.

Pramana enforces your compliance rules. It does not write your policies, appoint your Data Protection Officer, design your grievance process, or decide how your organisation treats personal data. Those decisions require a named person with actual authority and accountability.

If no one in your organisation is responsible for data protection, adding Pramana does not fix that. It gives the person responsible a much better tool — but the responsible person must exist first.

ON CLAIM HONESTY

Pramana cannot prove it prevented a fine.

A governance tool can document that a rule was enforced, that a decision was made correctly, and that evidence exists if questioned. It cannot prove a counterfactual — that without it, a complaint would have been filed, escalated, and penalised at a specific amount.

What Pramana can honestly claim: it closes documented governance gaps, and it produces the evidence your organisation needs to answer a regulatory investigation. Those are real and valuable outcomes. We will not claim more than that.

REGULATORY PERIMETER

What law Pramana covers — and what it does not.

Pramana’s knowledge graph currently covers the DPDP Act 2023, the DPDP Rules 2025, and selected sectoral obligations. Your business may have obligations under other laws that are not yet in the knowledge graph. These are listed below by sector.

Clinics & Healthcare
Covered
  • DPDP Act 2023 (all provisions)
  • DPDP Rules 2025
  • DPBI notification obligations
Not covered
  • Clinical Establishments Act 2010 record-retention rules
  • NMC telemedicine practice guidelines
  • State-level health data regulations
  • ABDM / Ayushman Bharat digital health standards
Schools & Coaching Centres
Covered
  • DPDP Act 2023 (all provisions, including child data & parental consent)
  • DPDP Rules 2025
Not covered
  • CBSE / state board data norms
  • NEP 2020 data provisions
  • RTE Act student-record obligations
  • NCERT digital content licensing rules
NBFCs, Fintech & Payments
Covered
  • DPDP Act 2023 (all provisions)
  • DPDP Rules 2025
  • Payment-data localisation (RBI) — S7 cross-border scenario
Not covered
  • Full RBI Master Directions on IT and cybersecurity
  • SEBI cybersecurity circular (full scope)
  • IRDAI data guidelines
  • PCI-DSS obligations
  • PMLA record-keeping requirements
Any Business
Covered
  • DPDP Act 2023 (all provisions)
  • DPDP Rules 2025
  • AI governance evidence (sector-independent)
Not covered
  • IT Act 2000 / SPDI Rules (except where DPDP supersedes)
  • Consumer Protection Act 2019 digital obligations
  • MeitY AI governance guidelines (in development)
  • Sector-specific regulations not listed above

The knowledge graph is updated as regulations develop. If your sector has specific obligations not listed here, contact us — expanding the KG for a specific regulatory framework is part of how custom deployments are scoped.

HONEST FIT CHECK

Who should not use Pramana.

We would rather lose a sale than have a customer use the product for the wrong thing.

  • Anyone who needs a compliance certificate or attestation — Pramana produces governance evidence, not certifications. If a procurement requirement or contract clause requires a specific certification, Pramana alone cannot satisfy it.
  • Anyone expecting legal advice — the tool encodes rules; it does not advise on legal strategy, dispute resolution, or regulatory negotiation.
  • Organisations without a named person accountable for data protection — the tool needs someone to own the policies it enforces. It cannot substitute for governance accountability.
  • Anyone whose primary compliance obligation is not DPDP — if your main constraint is full RBI IT governance, SEBI surveillance, or sector frameworks outside the current knowledge graph, Pramana will cover only part of your obligation set. Partial coverage applied uncritically is worse than acknowledged gaps.

If you are not sure whether Pramana fits your situation, tell us what you need. We will tell you honestly whether it does.

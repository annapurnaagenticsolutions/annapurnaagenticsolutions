PRAMANA · DATA PRINCIPAL RIGHTS

What your customers can ask for. What that means for you.

DPDP grants every individual four statutory rights against any business that holds their personal data. Pramana’s evidence ledger exists to make sure your business can actually answer each of these — not just intend to.

01
SECTION 11 · DPDP ACT 2023

Right to Access

A customer can request a summary of what personal data you hold about them, who you have shared it with, and why you are processing it. You must provide a real, complete answer.

What this looks like in practice

A customer emails: “What do you have on me?” You need a complete answer that covers every system where their data lives — not a guess based on whichever database someone remembers to check.

If your AI systems have been making decisions about this customer, each of those decisions involved their personal data. An access request asks you to account for all of it.

How Pramana helps

Every AI decision Pramana governs is recorded with the Data Principal’s identifier, the action, the rule applied, and the timestamp. When an access request arrives, the answer is already in the evidence ledger — a complete, ordered record of every AI decision that touched this person’s data, ready to retrieve.

02
SECTION 12 · DPDP ACT 2023

Right to Correction & Erasure

A customer can ask you to correct inaccurate personal data, or request deletion once the data is no longer needed for the purpose you originally collected it for.

What this looks like in practice

A customer asks you to delete their account and all associated data. The request has to reach every system their data lives in — not just the one they happened to contact you about. If an AI system used their data before the deletion, the evidence of that use may also need to be retained or purged depending on how the request is scoped.

An erasure request is not just a database delete. It is a process that must be documented, and its completion must be provable.

How Pramana helps

Pramana’s governance receipts record every AI action taken on a Data Principal’s data. When an erasure is requested, the ledger provides the complete map of what needs to be addressed — and after erasure, can record the deletion as a sealed event, proving the request was honoured.

03
SECTION 13 · DPDP ACT 2023

Right to Grievance Redressal

A customer can file a complaint about how their data was handled. Your business must respond within a defined window. If the complaint goes unanswered, it can be escalated directly to the Data Protection Board of India.

What this looks like in practice

This is a clock, not a suggestion. A grievance arrives. The clock starts. If your response doesn’t come within the required window — or if the customer is unsatisfied with it — they have a clear path to the DPBI.

The DPBI investigation that follows will ask: when did the complaint arrive? What did you respond? When? Do you have a record of it? A complaint that became a formal investigation because of a missed response window is avoidable — but only if you’re tracking the clock.

How Pramana helps

Pramana timestamps grievance receipt and routes it to the Grievance Officer queue. The response, and its timestamp, are sealed in the evidence record. If the matter goes to the DPBI, your evidence of receipt, response, and timeline is already documented — not reconstructed.

04
SECTION 14 · DPDP ACT 2023 · UNIQUE TO INDIA

Right to Nominate

A customer can designate another person to exercise all of their data rights — access, correction, erasure, grievance — in the event of their death or incapacity.

This right has no direct equivalent in GDPR or most other global privacy frameworks. It is distinctive to India’s DPDP Act.
What this looks like in practice

A verified nominee contacts your business on behalf of a deceased or incapacitated customer. They provide their nomination authority and ask to access, correct, or delete the customer’s data.

You need a process to verify the nomination — confirm who designated this person, that the designation was validly made, and that it covers the rights being exercised. Treating this as an edge case to resolve when it happens is not a plan.

How Pramana helps

If a nominee exercises a deceased customer’s access rights, Pramana’s ledger shows exactly what AI decisions were made about the customer’s data, when, and under what rule. The nominee gets a complete, verified record. The evidence of how it was provided is sealed.

TEAM DISCUSSION EXERCISE

Practise routing a privacy question to the right owners.

Use this short meeting exercise to clarify facts, check the current official position and assign follow-up. It is not an AI consultation or legal determination.

Leave with clear ownership

For each open point, record the accountable operational owner, the official source or evidence needed, and who should review it before a decision.

Scenario: a service changes its data use

A team wants to use information collected during a customer service interaction for a new business purpose. The details, roles and applicable facts are not yet fully established.
  1. What information is involved, and whose personal data is it?
  2. What purpose was originally communicated, and what new purpose is proposed?
  3. Who determines the purpose and means for each activity?
  4. Which official provisions are effective on the date being assessed?
  5. What facts, approvals or advice are still needed before a decision?

Optional scenario: education and learner activity

An education service is considering analytics that may involve children’s digital activity. The people, purpose, roles, data and applicable rules have not yet been confirmed.
  1. What age, data and purpose facts need confirmation?
  2. Have accountable people checked Section 9 and Rules 10 and 12, their commencement and any applicable conditions?
  3. Could separate education or sector-specific instruments apply?
  4. Which questions need a responsible human decision, and who owns the next step?

Discuss, do not assume

The next step depends on the actual facts and applicable law. This exercise intentionally gives no automated allow/block answer or legal conclusion.

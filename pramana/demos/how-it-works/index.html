A GOVERNANCE JOURNEY

From a processing question to an owned next step.

Step through a general approach teams can use to understand an activity, consult official sources and keep decisions accountable. This does not reveal Pramana’s internal implementation.

Walk through the process

Step 1 of 4

Pramana’s role

Pramana helps teams bring DPDP information and governance work into a clearer view, coordinate responsibilities and organise evidence. Accountable people remain responsible for decisions and qualified legal review.

See how Pramana helps →

A simple sequence

01 · UNDERSTAND

Describe the activity

Capture the purpose, people, data and organisations involved.

02 · CHECK

Read applicable sources

Review official text, commencement, conditions and relevant separate rules.

03 · ASSIGN

Clarify ownership

Route questions and decisions to accountable business, privacy, legal and technology owners.

04 · RECORD

Keep useful records

Document relevant decisions, actions and open questions in a suitable way.

Illustrative only

A GENERAL GOVERNANCE APPROACH

Turn privacy questions into owned work.

A practical governance discussion can move from understanding processing to checking official sources, clarifying responsibility and keeping useful records. This describes a general organisational approach for discussion.

A simple sequence

01 · UNDERSTAND

Describe the activity

Capture the purpose, people, data and organisations involved.

02 · CHECK

Read applicable sources

Review official text, commencement, conditions and relevant separate rules.

03 · ASSIGN

Clarify ownership

Route questions and decisions to accountable business, privacy, legal and technology owners.

04 · RECORD

Keep useful records

Document relevant decisions, actions and open questions in a suitable way.

Illustrative only

This general example does not define product scope or determine a legal outcome. Specific product details are discussed directly where appropriate.

How It Fits Into Your Business

PRAMANA runs inside your network, not in someone else's cloud. Here's what that means for you:

🔒
Your Data Never Leaves
PRAMANA runs on a machine inside your office or server room. No patient records, student data, or financial information is sent to any external server. An egress filter enforces this, even if a component tries to phone home, it's blocked. This matters for DPDP Sec 16 (cross-border transfer) compliance.
🔌
No External Dependencies
No external cloud APIs. The AI governance engine runs 100% locally on your private on-premise infrastructure. No monthly API bills. No surprise cost spikes. No vendor lock-in. Works on a standard desktop or mini-PC, no GPU required for the assessment mode.
🖥
Simple Web Interface
Access via your secure private local web interface. No client app installation required on staff devices.. No VPN. No firewall changes. Your compliance officer, your clinic manager, your school principal, anyone can use it from their existing laptop. Reports export as HTML or PDF.
📡
Air-Gap Friendly
For high-security environments (hospitals, banks, defense), PRAMANA can run fully air-gapped. Updates come as signed offline bundles, you transfer them via USB, the system verifies the cryptographic signature, then applies the update. No network connection needed, ever.
👥
Multi-User, Role-Based
Your compliance officer sees the full dashboard. Your department heads see only their sector's checklist. Your auditor gets a read-only report view. All access is logged with a tamper-evident audit trail, so you can prove who did what, when.
📦
One-Click Reports
Generate a Board-ready compliance report in one click. Includes: your compliance score, penalty exposure, gap analysis, remediation plan, AI system inventory, and regulatory mapping. Print it, email it, or hand it to your auditor. No formatting needed.

Start with facts

  • Map each activity separately.
  • Identify the purpose, relevant people and data involved.
  • Clarify the organisation’s role and relationships with other parties.
  • Check commencement, statutory scope, exemptions and separate sector requirements.
  • Bring unresolved questions to the appropriate accountable owner or adviser.

A scenario is not an applicability finding

Industry labels alone do not establish a legal role, exemption, Significant Data Fiduciary status or specific obligation. Use the official texts and assess actual facts.

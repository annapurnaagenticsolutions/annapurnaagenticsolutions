WHAT is the DPDP Act?

Statutory Framework

Passed by Parliament in August 2023, the Act creates rights for Data Principals (citizens) and legally binding duties for Data Fiduciaries (enterprises, clinics, schools, fintechs) processing digital personal data.

WHY is Compliance Urgent?

Statutory Penalty Matrix

Section 33 mandates statutory penalties up to ₹250 Crore per incident for security failures and up to ₹200 Crore for minor tracking breaches. Personal director liability and operating license risks apply.

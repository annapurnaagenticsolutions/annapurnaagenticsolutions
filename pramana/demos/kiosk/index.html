ASSISTED AND SELF-SERVICE CHANNELS

Consider the whole service journey.

Websites, mobile apps, ATMs, customer service points, kiosks, call centres and in-person assistance may connect different processing activities. This page provides general discovery prompts only.

Questions to map

What is collected?

Identify data requested or generated at each step, and whether each field is needed.

Why is it used?

Record the purposes for each activity and the people accountable for them.

Who else is involved?

Consider service providers, recipients, platform operators and role allocation.

What else applies?

For a covered payment-system provider or Aadhaar-related service, check relevant official RBI or UIDAI instruments; the service label alone does not settle scope.

Keep regulatory layers separate

DPDP and sector-specific obligations arise from distinct instruments. A sector label or technology choice does not automatically resolve which instrument applies. Verify the specific source and facts.

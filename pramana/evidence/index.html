GOVERNANCE EVIDENCE

Keep governance decisions understandable and reviewable.

Organisations may need clear records of how privacy responsibilities are handled. Pramana’s public description stays at the level of that need and does not disclose internal evidence formats or mechanisms.

Good governance records can help teams

Explain decisions

Keep a usable account of the context, responsibility and reasoning behind an organisational decision.

Support follow-up

Help teams find open questions, required actions, owners and review points.

Prepare for scrutiny

Organise relevant materials for internal review, audit preparation or a regulatory response, subject to the quality and context of the evidence.

Evidence has limits.

A record is not a compliance conclusion. Documentation cannot by itself establish that every legal duty applied, that underlying facts were complete, or that an external reviewer will assign it a particular weight. Applicability and compliance require context-specific human assessment.

Pramana does not claim statutory certification or guaranteed acceptance by a regulator, auditor or court.

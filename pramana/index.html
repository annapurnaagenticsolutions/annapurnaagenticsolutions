DIGITAL PERSONAL DATA · DPDP GOVERNANCE

DPDP starts with the data behind everyday services.

Everyday accounts, job applications, courses, payments and support requests may involve digital personal data. The DPDP Act recognizes the right of individuals to protect it alongside the need for lawful processing. Scope, exclusions, roles and commencement depend on the facts and relevant provisions.

Whether you use a service, run an organisation or build its technology, start with what data is handled and why.

DPDP BEYOND AI

DPDP questions reach beyond AI.

Websites, apps and everyday operations may involve digital personal data. AI and agent systems can be part of the picture, but they are not the whole picture. Questions may cover collection, use, sharing, storage, retention and erasure across websites, apps, APIs, SaaS, databases, service workflows and AI or agent systems.

  • Customer and service journeys
  • Workforce processes
  • Health and care services
  • Financial services and payment journeys
  • Education and enrolment
  • Support and grievance workflows
  • Service-provider relationships
  • Analytics, automation and AI

These are examples for orientation, not a conclusion that the Act applies to every organisation or activity.

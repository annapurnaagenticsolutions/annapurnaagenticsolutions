If your business uses people’s data, DPDP may apply.Pramana helps you get organised.

Names, phone numbers and messages can be personal data when kept digitally. India’s Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act recognises people’s right to protect it and the need to use it lawfully.

DPDP BEYOND AI

DPDP can matter even if you do not use AI.

A website, customer list, hiring form, payment flow or help desk may involve digital personal data. The questions can include what you collect, how you use and share it, how long you keep it and when you delete it. This can involve ordinary apps and business systems as well as AI or agents.

  • Customer and service journeys
  • Workforce processes
  • Health and care services
  • Financial services and payment journeys
  • Education and enrolment
  • Support and grievance workflows
  • Service-provider relationships
  • Analytics, automation and AI

These are examples to help you start thinking. They do not mean the Act applies to every business or activity.

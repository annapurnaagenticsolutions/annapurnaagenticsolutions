CORE CAPABILITIES

Three Layers of Deterministic Governance.

Moving from unchecked probabilistic LLM responses to verified statutory evidence.

01 · STATUTORY GROUNDING

Grounded Knowledge Layer

Every response is bound to primary statutory nodes (DPDP Act 2023, Rules 2025). Deterministic comparison catches phantom citations, numeric mismatches (72h vs 96h), and modal contradictions (may vs must) before synthesis.

02 · POLICY-AS-CODE

Governed Execution Gates

Dual-layer enforcement (YAML rule engine + Cedar policy authorization) validates actions before execution. Prevents unbundled consent violations, unauthorized data sharing, and cross-border data leakage.

03 · EVIDENCE LEDGER

Cryptographic Audit Trail

Every governance decision — whether approved, blocked, or held for human review — generates a sealed evidence receipt with SHA-256 hash chaining and exportable audit packs for regulatory oversight.

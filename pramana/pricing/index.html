PRAMANA · PRICING

Structured engagement. Not self-serve.

Pramana is in private beta. Deployments are configured specifically for your AI systems, your sector, and your compliance obligations. There is no generic pricing tier because there is no generic deployment.

HOW IT WORKS

Three things to know about how Pramana is priced.

LICENSING MODEL

Royalty license — not SaaS.

Pramana is licensed as intellectual property, not sold as a subscription service. You pay an annual license fee for the right to deploy and operate the system within your own infrastructure. There are no per-seat fees, no per-transaction fees, and no usage-based billing.

The license includes the governance engine, the knowledge graph for your applicable regulatory frameworks, and the evidence ledger system. Knowledge graph maintenance for the first year is included in the base license.

DEPLOYMENT MODEL

Customer-hosted. Single-tenant. Your infrastructure.

Pramana runs inside your own infrastructure — on-premise or private cloud. There is no multi-tenant option. Your data, governance decisions, and evidence records never leave your deployment boundary. Zero telemetry. Zero phone-home.

This means your costs are predictable: the license fee is fixed, and your infrastructure costs are whatever you already pay to run your own servers. There is no surprise usage bill.

FOUNDING BUYER TERMS

Early adopters get structured terms.

Pramana is in its first deployment cycle. Organisations taking a private beta deployment now — before full enforcement on 13 May 2027 — are eligible for founding buyer terms: structured pricing, priority knowledge-graph maintenance, and direct engagement with the engineering team during onboarding.

Founding buyer terms are available on a limited basis. Once the private beta closes, standard commercial terms apply.

WHY NOT SELF-SERVE?

Because the right configuration depends on your business.

Pramana’s governance policies, knowledge graph scope, and integration points are configured for your specific AI systems, your sector’s regulatory obligations, and your organisation’s risk profile. A generic “start free” tier would produce a misconfigured deployment that gives false confidence — which is worse than no governance at all.

The engagement starts with a conversation: what AI systems do you run, what personal data do they touch, what sector-specific obligations apply, and what evidence does your compliance team need. From there, we configure Pramana for your actual situation.

That conversation is free and comes with no obligation.

IF WE CEASE TO OPERATE

What we commit to our customers.

Pramana is a customer-hosted product. Your governance evidence records, your rules, and your evidence ledger all live on your infrastructure. Annapurna does not hold your data — which means if we cease to operate, your records are unaffected.

We commit additionally: if Annapurna Agentic Solutions ceases operations, active licenses convert to perpetual read-only status automatically. You retain full access to your evidence ledger and your sealed records indefinitely. Knowledge graph maintenance will continue for a minimum of 12 months from the date of any cessation notice, managed by a named escrow arrangement (details provided at contract stage for founding-buyer engagements).

The regulatory record you built with Pramana is yours. A vendor relationship ending should not mean a governance record disappearing.

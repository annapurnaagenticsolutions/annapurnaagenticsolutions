LAYER ONE — STATUTORY GROUNDING

Every AI claim about the law is verified against primary sources.

AI systems hallucinate. When they hallucinate about law — citing provisions that do not exist, misquoting obligations, inventing statutory references — the downstream consequence is a legal liability for your organisation.

Pramana’s first layer checks every AI-generated statutory claim against a structured, versioned knowledge graph built from primary Gazette publications. The check is deterministic — not probabilistic re-evaluation by another AI. The knowledge graph version used for each check is recorded in the evidence receipt, so the basis for every decision is permanently traceable.

If the AI cites a provision that does not exist in the knowledge graph, the output is held. A phantom statute never reaches your customer.

LAYER TWO — POLICY ENFORCEMENT

Your compliance rules are enforced before any action executes — not audited after.

After the statutory check, the proposed action is evaluated against your organisation’s defined policy set. Policies are expressed declaratively and are formally verifiable — before deployment, you can prove that a policy set is internally consistent, covers every action category, and cannot be bypassed by edge-case inputs.

The outcome of every policy evaluation is one of three dispositions: approved (action proceeds), blocked (action is denied and a receipt is issued), or held for human review (action waits in a reviewer queue until a named individual approves or rejects it). All three dispositions produce an evidence receipt.

Every compliance rule you define becomes a hard enforcement boundary — not a recommendation, not a post-hoc audit finding.

LAYER THREE — EVIDENCE SEALING

Every governance decision leaves a tamper-evident, auditor-readable receipt.

Whether a decision was approved, blocked, or sent for human review — a cryptographically signed receipt is issued. Receipts form an integrity-verifiable chain: each receipt contains a verifiable reference to the one before it. Any gap in the chain, or any alteration to a historical receipt, is immediately detectable without access to the production system.

Receipts record: the action requested, the statutory provisions checked (with knowledge graph version), the policy rules applied (with policy version), the outcome, the timestamp at nanosecond precision, and — for human-review decisions — the identity of the reviewer and the time of their decision.

When the DPBI asks “show us what your AI decided” — this chain is your answer. It cannot be reconstructed after the fact. It can only be produced if it was created at decision time.

SYSTEM GUARANTEES

Five properties a technical evaluator should test.

These are not design aspirations. They are verifiable properties. We encourage evaluators to attempt to break them in a private test environment.

GUARANTEE 1 — DETERMINISM

The same input always produces the same governance decision.

Pramana’s governance layer is not probabilistic. It does not re-evaluate decisions using a language model. Given an identical action request, an identical knowledge graph state, and an identical policy set — the decision will always be identical. This is a fundamental requirement for legal evidence: a decision that might be different if re-run is not a decision you can cite in proceedings.

GUARANTEE 2 — CHAIN INTEGRITY

Any tampering with historical receipts is detectable.

Evidence receipts form a cryptographically linked chain. If any receipt in the sequence is altered, deleted, or replaced — even a single bit — the chain integrity check fails. This is verifiable by any third party with the public verification key, without access to the production system or the private signing material.

GUARANTEE 3 — OFFLINE VERIFIABILITY

Evidence bundles can be verified without system access.

The sealed evidence bundle you export for a regulatory submission can be cryptographically verified by the DPBI, an auditor, or a court — without them needing access to your production system, your private signing material, or any live component of Pramana. The bundle is self-contained and verifiable in isolation.

GUARANTEE 4 — FORMAL POLICY VERIFICATION

Policy completeness and non-bypassability are provable before deployment.

Pramana uses a policy engine with formal analysis tooling. Before a policy set goes to production, you can prove — mathematically, not through testing — that the policy set covers every action category you intend to govern, that no two rules contradict each other, and that there is no combination of inputs that would allow an action to bypass the policy enforcement layer. This is a stronger guarantee than test coverage.

GUARANTEE 5 — ZERO EXTERNAL CALLS

No data leaves your infrastructure. Ever.

Pramana makes no external network calls during operation — no telemetry, no phone-home, no cloud API calls, no external model inference. Governance decisions are made entirely within your own infrastructure, using only the knowledge graph and policy rules you have deployed. This is not a privacy policy. It is a verifiable architectural property: there is no outbound connection in the system.

GUARANTEE 6 — KNOWLEDGE GRAPH VERSIONING

Every decision is traceable to the exact statutory source used.

The knowledge graph is versioned. Every governance receipt records which version of the graph was used at decision time. If the DPDP Rules are updated, you update the graph, increment the version, and deploy. All prior decisions remain traceable to the exact statutory text that governed them at the time — so your evidence record does not become ambiguous when the law changes.

INTEGRATION APPROACH

In-process middleware. Not a proxy. Not a separate service.

Pramana is designed to be the smallest possible addition to your existing AI system — one that adds governance without changing anything your users experience.

INTEGRATION PATTERN — INTERCEPT BEFORE ACTION

Wrap the action-dispatch layer, not the AI model itself.

You do not replace your AI model. You do not change your user interface. Pramana integrates at the point where your AI’s intent translates into a real-world action — a database write, an API call, a notification, a decision record. Before the action executes, it passes through the governance gate. If approved, it proceeds. If blocked or held, the gate returns the disposition and a sealed receipt; your existing code handles the fallback.

Full API specification — method signatures, request/response schemas, error handling, and timeout behaviour — is available under NDA.

INTEGRATION PATTERN — CLAIM VERIFICATION

Pipe LLM output through the statutory checker before it reaches the user.

For AI systems that generate customer-facing responses containing regulatory language, Pramana provides a claim-verification integration point. The LLM’s output is passed through before delivery. Any statutory reference is matched against the knowledge graph. If a provision does not exist or is materially misapplied, the output is held and a clean fallback is returned.

This pattern requires no change to how your LLM is prompted or invoked — it intercepts the output, not the input.

INTEGRATION PATTERN — HUMAN REVIEW QUEUE

Significant decisions require a named human to approve before they execute.

Certain decision categories — credit denial, data deletion, cross-system transfers, any action involving a minor’s data — can be configured to require explicit human approval before execution. The action is held in a reviewer queue. A named individual reviews the AI’s recommendation, approves or rejects it, and that human decision (including the reviewer’s identity and timestamp) is sealed into the evidence chain alongside the AI’s original recommendation.

The DPBI audit record then shows not just what the AI recommended, but who reviewed it and when.

PERFORMANCE

Governance that runs in the background — invisibly.

DECISION LATENCY

Under 50ms per governance decision (p99)

The full three-layer pipeline — statutory grounding check, policy evaluation, and evidence receipt generation — completes in under 50 milliseconds at the 99th percentile on reference hardware. Users and customers experience no change in response time.

Latency scales predictably with policy set complexity. The primary driver is policy evaluation; knowledge graph lookups and receipt generation are constant-time operations for most decision types.

KNOWLEDGE GRAPH

Versioned statutory graph — DPDP Act, IT Act, sector schedules

The primary knowledge graph covers the DPDP Act 2023 (all sections), DPDP Rules 2025, IT Act 2000 (data-relevant provisions), NITI Aayog AI principles, OECD AI Recommendations, ISO/IEC 42001 clauses, and NIST AI RMF categories. An extended graph covering additional frameworks is under active development.

The graph is versioned independently of the policy engine. Knowledge graph updates are deployed without requiring a system restart or policy set recompilation.

POLICY ENGINE

Declarative, formally verifiable — not rule-weight tuning

Policies are expressed declaratively in a structured format. The engine supports formal analysis — before deployment, you can verify that your policy set is complete (covers every intended action category), consistent (no two rules contradict each other), and non-bypassable (no edge-case input can skip enforcement).

This is fundamentally different from ML-based anomaly detection or weighted rule engines — the outcome of a policy evaluation is provable from the policy text alone, without running the system.

# v3.0 — Experience Reconstruction

## Why this release exists
v2.x accumulated substantial adaptive, causal and emergent behavior, but screenshots showed that much of the public surface still read as conventional website UI: separate sections, card grids, inspector panels and a corner assistant.

v3.0 does not replace the living engine. It reconstructs the presentation so visitors can perceive that engine.

## Home — one persistent world
The opening experience uses one spatial ecosystem across three directed scroll beats:

1. **Establish** — portfolio proposition + wide ecosystem.
2. **Focus** — selected world moves forward; relationships and micro-scenes become the primary interaction.
3. **Journey** — the same spatial environment becomes the connected route visualization rather than cutting to a separate scene.

The final CTA is still a separate third public act, but visually inherits the active world.

## Explore — spatial atlas
The six worlds no longer present primarily as a 2×3 card grid. They occupy one field around the Annapurna core. Selection brings the active world and its existing connected neighbors forward while unrelated worlds recede.

## Interactive Lab — single theatre
Runtime Integrity, AXON Structure and Website Studio no longer compete as three equal cards. One interaction fills the theatre at a time; the three model selectors act as scene choices.

## Impact typography
No semantic impact word is underlined. Depth is communicated through:
- perspective/Z translation;
- selective foreground shadow;
- focus/saturation;
- a soft volumetric field behind the glyphs.

Under `prefers-reduced-motion`, the depth transform flattens.

## Adaptive/agentic behavior
The adaptive context remains available to the Page Guide and decision system but is no longer rendered as a debugging ribbon. The Guide can also activate Lab scenes directly.

## Preserved v2.x engine
v3.0 retains:
- deterministic adaptive structure;
- local return-state continuity;
- temporal state;
- real-state current;
- micro-scenes;
- cross-world consequences;
- emergent composites;
- evidence/truth boundaries.

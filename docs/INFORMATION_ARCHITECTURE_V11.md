# Information Architecture v1.1

## Home
Purpose: understand Annapurna in under ~30 seconds.
- positioning statement
- interactive six-world ecosystem
- four small proof signals
- three capability families
- one living/public pulse
- primary CTAs

## Explore
Purpose: understand the portfolio.
- six selectable product worlds
- focused inspector panel
- direct links to existing product hubs

## Living Lab
Purpose: experience ideas.
- Runtime Integrity rehearsal
- AXON workflow-structure rehearsal
- Website Studio presentation rehearsal

## Evidence
Purpose: inspect claims.
- Evidence Atlas from `evidence-manifest.json`
- public repository signals
- semantic public-state history

This separation intentionally prevents the landing page from becoming a scroll-heavy product dump.

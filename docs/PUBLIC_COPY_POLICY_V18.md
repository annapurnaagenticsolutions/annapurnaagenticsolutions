# Public Copy Policy — v1.8

Visitor-facing pages must not explain internal design methodology unless the visitor explicitly asks the Page Guide for an explanation of their current route.

## Prohibited market-page phrases
Examples include:
- “Why living”;
- “Not more animation”;
- “A living website should…”;
- “Rules-based”;
- “Deterministic rehearsal” / “Workflow rehearsal” / “Presentation rehearsal”;
- “On-device rules first”;
- “The normal static website remains underneath.”

## Allowed locations
The concepts may appear in engineering documentation, contracts, tests and audit reports.

## Why
The public website should communicate value through product behavior, spatial response and clear outcomes. Architecture explanation is supporting evidence, not homepage content.

`scripts/public_copy_audit.py` enforces this boundary across Home, Explore, Interactive Lab and Evidence.

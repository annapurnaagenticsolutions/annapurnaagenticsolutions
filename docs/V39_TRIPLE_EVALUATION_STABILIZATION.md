# v4.0 — Triple Evaluation Stabilization

## Why this release exists
v3.8 established the right direction for selective glass and Evidence Field semantics. Before requesting another screenshot cycle, v4.0 performs three separate audits so visual iteration is not driven by one screenshot defect at a time.

## Evaluation 1 — typography and visual rhythm
The family remains unchanged. Home retains one intentional Display role; Explore, Lab and Evidence share one page-H1 role; Focus/Journey/Lab scenes share one major-H2 role; support headings share one H3 role. Navigation, controls, intro spacing and responsive bounds are normalized so consistency comes from semantic roles rather than forcing every heading to the same optical size.

## Evaluation 2 — optical material and cost
Selective glass remains beneficial, but desktop blur is reduced and the sticky-header blur is disabled on mobile. Large topology/world stages remain unblurred. Evidence cards no longer use hover-lift motion, preventing the provenance surface from drifting toward generic SaaS-card behavior.

## Evaluation 3 — continuity and evidence comprehension
The Home exit now exposes the visitor's current local trail so Journey does not terminate into a generic CTA. Evidence declares its inspection grammar—Claim → Source → Scope → Limit/conflict—and each claim labels its evidence note. These are comprehension devices, not additional product claims.

## Boundaries
No new framework, runtime, third-party dependency, WebGL context, network call, product maturity claim or feature family. Existing v3.8 fallback data, v3.5 spatial governance, v3.4 Journey ownership, v3.3 motion grammar, accessibility and truth contracts remain authoritative.

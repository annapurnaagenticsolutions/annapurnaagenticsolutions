# Semantic Depth Typography — v3.0

Impact words must not resemble links.

## Rule
Impact words are never underlined.

## Allowed emphasis
- small Z-axis translation;
- soft volumetric shadow;
- context-linked color shift;
- selective saturation;
- subtle perspective response.

## Selection
Only semantically relevant words respond to the active world or narrative beat. Emphasis should remain sparse; the visual system is intended to foreground meaning rather than decorate every headline.

## Accessibility
Reduced-motion removes the transform and depth shadow. Text remains normal readable HTML and retains no interaction requirement.

# v2.4 — Cross-world Causality

## Objective
Move beyond six isolated micro-scenes. A completed interaction in one world now leaves a temporary consequence in related worlds, so the shared ecosystem can carry cause-and-effect across product boundaries.

## Behavior
- Complete a 3-step micro-scene.
- Two related worlds receive a subtle afterglow and a moving cross-world current.
- Enter one of those worlds deliberately.
- Its micro-scene acknowledges the incoming state.
- The first interaction integrates that state.
- Completing the target micro-scene propagates a new pair of consequences.

This creates an interruptible chain such as:
`AI Solutions → AXON → Software Lab → Idea Hub`.

## Constraints
Consequences exist only in page memory. Reloading clears them. They cannot alter product truth, Evidence, semantic history, local visitor profile, or maturity.

Causal targets are constrained to the existing `interaction-model.json` connection graph; v2.4 does not invent new product relationships.

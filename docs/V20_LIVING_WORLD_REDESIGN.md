# v2.0 Living World Redesign

## Why the reset
v1.8–v1.9 improved immersion, but screenshots still read as a sophisticated SaaS/dashboard composition: world cards, capability cards, metric cards and floating detail panels. v2.0 changes the public presentation grammar while preserving the working adaptive engine beneath it.

## Design decisions
1. Home has exactly three public acts: ecosystem, journey, next step.
2. Selected-world detail sits with narrative copy, never over the spatial scene.
3. Product worlds are points in space, not rectangular mini-cards.
4. Real public data remains available in Evidence; on Home it should influence state/behavior rather than appear as a metrics table.
5. One world accent persists across hero, journey and CTA.
6. The connected path is scroll-progressive and manually interruptible.
7. White remains the base; depth comes from topology, light, faint grids, paths and motion rather than dark cinematic surfaces.
8. No new framework, WebGL context, external font, shader or autoplay audio is introduced.
9. Reduced-motion keeps state changes but removes animated choreography.
10. The page agent remains an environment controller rather than the central experience.

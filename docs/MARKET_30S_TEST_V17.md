# Synthetic First-30-Seconds Market Test — v1.7

This is a deterministic heuristic test, **not empirical user research**.

| Persona | 5s expectation | 15s action | 30s reachable outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| Enterprise AI buyer | AI + governed systems are clear | Choose Enterprise AI | AI products, evidence, Contact |
| AI architect/evaluator | infrastructure + inspectability are clear | Enterprise view / map | AXON, Living Lab, Evidence |
| Learning visitor | learning products are explicit | Choose Learning | WonderHub, Lab, Contact |
| MSME operator | practical tools are visible | Choose MSME tools | Idea Hub, product detail, Contact |
| Design prospect | digital experiences are explicit | Choose Design & web | Website Studio, Lab, Contact |
| General referral | company breadth is understandable | Explore all / map | Explore, Living Lab, Contact |

## v1.6 friction identified
- positioning was conceptually strong but commercially vague;
- the hero exposed too many diagnostic details before the visitor interacted;
- repository/local/temporal metrics duplicated information below;
- the ecosystem demonstrated liveness but did not explicitly guide traversal between related products.

## v1.7 correction
The hero is simpler, adaptation is summarized rather than narrated, and the interactive map now offers a direct connected-world next step.

## Remaining unknowns
- real scan order and comprehension time;
- actual mobile ergonomics across devices;
- deployed LCP/INP;
- whether market visitors prefer the adaptive chooser or ignore it;
- whether Contact becomes visible soon enough for high-intent buyers.

Those require deployed browser and human testing.

# Optical Material Contract v3.8

1. Glass indicates focus, inspection, guidance, or provenance; never wallpaper.
2. Do not blur the full Home stage, Explore atlas field, Lab theatre, or page background.
3. Home has a dedicated Display role; Explore/Lab/Evidence share the inner-page H1 role.
4. Major experiential H2 and support-heading tiers remain shared.
5. Essential information must remain legible with blur/transparency disabled.
6. `file://` preview must render evidence/repository/history data through a static generated fallback.
7. New material treatment may not introduce a framework, WebGL context, third-party runtime, or network dependency.

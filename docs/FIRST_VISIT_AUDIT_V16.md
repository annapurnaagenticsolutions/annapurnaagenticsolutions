# First-Visit Audit — v1.6

## Heuristic question
Can a visitor understand that the site is alive without already knowing the implementation?

## v1.5 weakness
- adaptive rules were mostly invisible;
- return/temporal signals existed but were scattered;
- page-level agency lived in a floating launcher;
- cross-page continuity was behavioral but not visibly acknowledged.

## v1.6 target
Within the hero, the visitor can see: what the page sensed, what it changed, and how to influence it. After navigating, meaningful context is carried forward visibly.

## Still intentionally restrained
No autoplay spectacle, no cursor tricks, no audio, no shader background, no 3D dependency.

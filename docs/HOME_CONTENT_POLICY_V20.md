# Home Content Policy — v2.0

Home exists to create understanding and movement, not to enumerate everything Annapurna owns.

## Keep on Home
- clear company/product scope;
- the six-world ecosystem;
- intent entry portals;
- one selected-world narrative;
- one connected portfolio journey;
- two or three high-value next actions.

## Move elsewhere
- repository counts and issue/commit tables → Evidence;
- product inventories → Explore;
- simulations and detailed interactions → Interactive Lab;
- provenance and maturity reconciliation → Evidence;
- implementation methodology → engineering documentation.

## Presentation rule
If a piece of information can be experienced as behavior, do not first explain it as a feature.

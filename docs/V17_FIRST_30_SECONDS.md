# v1.7 — First 30 Seconds Design

## Objective
Improve market comprehension and perceived liveness without expanding the four-page information architecture.

## Changes
1. Rewrite the H1 and first paragraph around what Annapurna actually builds.
2. Keep intent selection visible, but phrase it as a visitor question rather than a system control.
3. Collapse the detailed Sense/Decide/Respond receipt into one adaptive-status line; deeper reasoning remains one click away.
4. Remove duplicated hero metrics and temporal explanation from the visual foreground.
5. Make the Living ecosystem itself do more work through guided portfolio connections.
6. Give the Page Guide the same `followConnection()` tool.
7. Preserve all v1.6 adaptive, temporal, sensory, truth, privacy and reduced-motion boundaries.

## Design principle
**Liveness should reduce navigation effort, not add explanation overhead.**

# Material Truth Contract — v3.2

The Material Living Layer is a presentation response, not evidence of product execution.

It may:
- translate a deliberate world selection into particles, depth and atmospheric response;
- form a visual grammar associated with the selected product world;
- temporarily cross the ecosystem/copy boundary;
- react to directed scroll beats;
- settle back into the shared environment.

It may not:
- imply that a production product invoked another product;
- change product maturity or public state;
- write Evidence Atlas claims;
- create semantic-history milestones;
- persist material completion or particles in localStorage;
- infer identity or a visitor profile.

The effect is intentionally ephemeral and disappears after settling or page reload.

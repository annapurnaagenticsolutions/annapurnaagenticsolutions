# Composite Truth Contract — v2.5

Emergent composites may visualize a meaningful combination of existing connected product concepts.

They may:
- reconfigure the temporary visual topology;
- close an animated current around three worlds;
- recompose the local journey and semantic scene word;
- respond to an inherited causal chain.

They may not:
- claim live cross-product orchestration;
- persist unlocks across page loads;
- change product maturity or evidence;
- write semantic history;
- create a visitor profile.

All sequence edges must already exist in `interaction-model.json`.

# v2.2 — Semantic Depth & World Signatures

## Objective
Increase impact without adding more sections or generic animation. Important words may appear to move toward the visitor only when their meaning is relevant to the active world or scroll phase. Each product world also gets a distinct short physical signature.

## Semantic depth words
- **AI systems** responds to AI / AXON context.
- **learning products** responds to WonderHub and learning-adjacent context.
- **digital experiences** responds to Website Studio.
- **connected** carries the largest depth during the connection phase.
- **working experience** receives a restrained lift near the final CTA.

The effect is pointer/scroll/world-state linked. There is no autonomous bouncing. `prefers-reduced-motion` flattens all depth transforms; low-power mode reduces them substantially.

## Six world signatures
1. AI Solutions — network nodes propagate.
2. WonderHub — concept constellation orbits.
3. Idea Hub — practical city structures rise.
4. AXON — typed packets move through lanes.
5. Website Studio — experience frames separate and morph.
6. Software Lab — experimental grid cells activate.

Selecting a world retriggers its signature. Hover/focus may preview the grammar, but deliberate selection produces the strongest event.

## Boundary
These effects never modify product maturity, evidence, semantic history, repository state, or adaptive intent. They are presentation responses only.

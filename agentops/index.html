Conceptual workflow

Questions to consider across an agent lifecycle.

1

Intake

Clarify the business need, owner, data, expected outcome, and level of autonomy.

2

Review

Consider suitability, risk, data readiness, safeguards, and human review.

3

Define controls

Plan policies for tools, data, environments, identity, and secrets.

4

Observe

Decide what traces, decisions, and evidence a real system should record.

5

Reassess

Use tested evidence and accountable review before any real-world deployment.

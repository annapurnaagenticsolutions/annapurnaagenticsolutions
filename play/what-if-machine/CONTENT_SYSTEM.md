# Content System — v1.3

The product is structured around five content layers:

1. **Prompt spark** — one impossible, visual, harmless question.
2. **Comedy logic** — inversion, tiny bureaucracy, fake science, social drama, or escalation.
3. **World logic** — one rule, one place, one authority, one running gag.
4. **Reusable format** — comic, poster, WhatsApp status, classroom task, creator caption, fake report, or series seed.
5. **Quality pass** — clarity, specificity, shareability, repeatability, and safety.

## Share-card system

Each strong result should be reducible to one of these cards:

- WhatsApp status
- Poster headline
- Fake report
- Comic cover
- Classroom spark
- Family game
- Creator reel
- Series seed

## Safety stance

Harmless absurdity only. Avoid real-person ridicule, politics, caste/community/religion jokes, adult content, violence, bullying, medical/legal/financial fake advice, and unsafe instructions.

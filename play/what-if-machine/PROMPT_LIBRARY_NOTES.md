v1.3 intentionally adds curation layers rather than random volume:

- `Stable showcase best 100` — demo-ready prompts.
- `Recurring character universe` — prompts that introduce repeatable cast members.
- `Share-card ready final prompts` — short, visual prompts for status/poster use.
- `Hinglish showcase samples` — Hinglish-friendly seeds for language testing.

## Quality criteria for future prompts

A new prompt should pass at least four of these five checks:

1. One clear subject.
2. One visual setting.
3. One impossible but safe twist.
4. Can become a punchline in under 14 words.
5. Can produce a sequel or alternate ending.

Do not expand prompt count again until real users identify gaps.

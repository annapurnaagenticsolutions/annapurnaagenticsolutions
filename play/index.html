The platform contract

Play stays open. Boundaries stay visible.

We are building the infrastructure around the experiences gradually, so a free first session never depends on an account, advertising or hidden data collection.

A

Enter without friction.

Start as a guest and reach the first meaningful interaction before we ask for anything.

B

Keep progress yours.

Local saves are the default. Cloud continuity will be an explicit value exchange, not a prerequisite to play.

C

Ship in honest layers.

Static showcase, preview backend and full product are different release states — and the labels stay visible.

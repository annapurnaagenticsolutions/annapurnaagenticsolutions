# Paheli Bazaar Showcase Guide

## One-minute pitch

Paheli Bazaar is a playful puzzle market where every shopkeeper gives a different type of challenge: riddles, emojis, logic, wordplay, mythology clues, science puzzles, and wrong-answer comedy. It is bounded, kid-safe by design, offline-ready, and mobile-first.

## What to demonstrate

- Bazaar metaphor: 7 shops instead of a generic quiz list.
- Replay depth: 140 curated puzzles with unseen-first selection.
- Family use: quick rounds and pass-the-phone play.
- Safety: no login, no backend, no open-ended unsafe chat, no personal data collection.
- Kid Mode: easier play by avoiding tricky puzzles.
- Learning value: science and wordplay explanations without feeling like an exam.

## Suggested demo path

1. Start at the gate and enter the bazaar.
2. Show the shop grid and explain each shop in one sentence.
3. Enter Logic Laddoo Shop and solve a puzzle.
4. Use a hint once to show reward reduction.
5. Try Wrong Answer Darbar to show comedy mode.
6. Open Daily Challenge and solve it to show the daily bonus.
7. Open Bazaar Pass to show coins, goals, titles, progress, Kid Mode, and recent rounds.

## Safe boundaries

The app avoids politics, caste/community jokes, adult content, violent jokes, unsafe experiments, religious argumentation, login, payments, ads, analytics, and personal data collection.

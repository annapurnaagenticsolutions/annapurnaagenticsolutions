THE PRODUCT HYPOTHESIS

Make a software expectation replayable.

For a check, supply a typed contract and named observations. The Rust engine returns a bounded report with the outcome and source dependencies.

A

Observe explicit values

Collectors and integrations provide UI, API, or workflow observations. The evaluator does not browse a URL or infer what a screenshot means.

≠

Compare the relationship

A typed contract describes the expected relationship, such as a displayed total matching line items.

?

Keep uncertainty visible

Missing or ambiguous inputs produce UNKNOWN. A report fingerprint identifies normalized input; it is not a signature or proof of truth.
